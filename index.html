About

Where quantum ideas meet artistic exploration.

EntangleTune explores new forms of musical creativity inspired by quantum computing concepts such as superposition, entanglement, and measurement.

The initiative aims to foster new connections between quantum technology and musical expression through research, artistic exploration, and public engagement.
